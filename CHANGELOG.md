# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-09-27

First public release.

- **Name the note**: a fret lights up and sounds; tap its name.
- **Find the note**: play the shown note within a hand position, optionally a specific octave.
- **Find them all**: play every octave of a note, in a position or across the neck.
- **Intervals**: play the requested interval above a highlighted root.
- **Tuner** with mic calibration.
- **Progress** screen: fretboard accuracy heatmap, per-note and per-interval accuracy and time.
- Timing modes (untimed, seconds per note, tempo with metronome and auto speed-up),
  sessions of 10/20/50 notes or endless, and adaptive practice that repeats missed notes.
- 16 frets, standard and alternate tunings, right- or left-handed.

Requires Android 8.0+. Signed with the project release key: if you installed an earlier
test build, uninstall it first (this clears its saved stats).
